## 1. Goal and desired conditions

People can obtain and understand reasons for public decisions, identify who is responsible and request review. Responsible bodies answer for decisions, correct errors and make the response traceable.

## 2. What Natalie Weber means by public transparency and accountability

The risk dossier covers open data, auditability, complaint procedures, whistleblower protection and responsibility for results. The terminology guide asks whether institutions deserve trust through fair, correctable procedures. Reported trust, service satisfaction and the ability to verify decisions describe different things.

## 3. Why the goal matters

Information helps people notice an error only when they can locate and interpret it. An effective complaint route gives them a way to obtain a response. Accountability gives a reviewing body the power and duty to act. In an Indonesian road-project experiment, a higher announced probability of government audit reduced the gap between reported and independently estimated spending. Effects of increased community participation were less clear in that setting. [Olken, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1086/517935)

## 4. How failures can spread

A decision without understandable reasons can hide who bears additional costs. People may fail to object before deadlines. Unreviewed errors can accumulate and make later participation less worthwhile. Oversight and an actual response can interrupt this path. Mere release of a document does not demonstrate these effects.

## 5. UN targets and the WÖk contribution

Targets 16.5 and 16.6 address corruption and effective, accountable and transparent institutions. Indicator 16.5.1 tracks reported bribery among people in contact with public officials. Indicator 16.6.1 measures budget execution against approved budgets, while 16.6.2 measures satisfaction with public services. Indicator 16.10.2 tracks countries that adopt and implement guarantees for public access to information. The WÖk approach follows particular decisions from usable disclosure through review and correction. The dossier additionally examines auditability, responsibilities, complaints and follow-up across institutions and relevant organisations. [UN SDG 16](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal16) [UN indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/)

## 6. People, planet and democracy

Accessible decisions can help residents question unequal burdens. In environmental monitoring, understandable methods, deadlines and results can make it possible to challenge a decision that shifts harm downstream. Any change in access, oversight or the environmental outcome needs its own evidence.

## 7. Relationships with other goals

Revealing decision reasons supports democracy quality when residents have meaningful ways to question them. Independent media can interpret records and investigate discrepancies. Review mechanisms also depend on rights and institutional independence. The same correction can have several goal references and should be counted once.

## 8. Application example

A hypothetical municipality publishes the initial cost of a road project, the award criteria and reasons for a later increase. An independent reviewer investigates a complaint. The municipality publishes the reasons for its response, subject to applicable protections. Download counts capture only one step in this sequence.

## 9. Observation and assessment

MasterItem `WOK-G-244` denotes country-by-country reporting on a 0 to 1 scale under R08. It applies to one corporate reporting question and requires criteria-based assurance. A public decision process may instead define usable and timely responses as a state variable, observe response times and review outcomes, and use a Reality Check to verify correction. The register's case mappings remain open. [Master register v1.5](https://wirkungsoekonomie.de/bibliothek/woek-master-items-register/)

## 10. Non-compensation and Reverse Merit Order

High disclosure volume cannot compensate for a verified hard breach of an applicable protection limit, such as an unlawful release of protected personal data. Identify the legal rule and apply the consequences of the relevant WÖk method and materiality assessment. Reverse Merit Order makes materially critical deficits visible and can limit the overall assessment under the applicable method.

## 11. How to achieve and sustain the goal

Publish intelligible reasons, procedures, dates and contacts. Assign independent review where appropriate. Track responses and remedies, apply safeguards to private data, and check whether affected people can use the process.
